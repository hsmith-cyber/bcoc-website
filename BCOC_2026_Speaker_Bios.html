Closing Keynote Speaker
Chief Michael J. Monds
Chief of Fire, Syracuse Fire Department (NY) · 2023 NYS Fire Chief of the Year
FRI9:30 – 11:00 AMClosing Keynote
Chief Michael J. Monds is the Chief of Fire for the Syracuse (NY) Fire Department and a recognized fire service executive with 26 years of public safety experience. He has served as Fire Chief for nine years under two mayoral administrations and is the first Black Fire Chief in the department’s 150-year history. He leads nearly 400 firefighters and civilian personnel protecting the City of Syracuse and the Central New York region. Under his leadership the department achieved the prestigious ISO Class 1 Public Protection Classification, modernized its apparatus fleet and operations, expanded leadership development, and made historic progress in the recruitment, retention, and promotion of women and firefighters of color. Named the 2023 New York State Fire Chief of the Year — the first Syracuse chief to receive the distinction — Chief Monds also serves as a public safety consultant, executive recruiter, and leadership mentor, and believes the greatest legacy any fire chief can leave is preparing others to lead.

Dr. Lori Moore-Merrell, DrPH, MPH
Principal Fire Safety Strategist, UL Research Institutes / Fire Safety Research Institute · Former U.S. Fire Administrator (2021–2025)
THU9:30 – 10:15 AMFeatured Address
Dr. Lori Moore-Merrell is the Principal Fire Safety Strategist at UL Research Institutes’ Fire Safety Research Institute and served as the presidentially appointed U.S. Fire Administrator from 2021 to 2025. As Administrator she oversaw the launch of the National Emergency Response Information System (NERIS), advanced the Fire Service One Voice initiative, chaired the Wildland Fire Management and Mitigation Commission, and convened the inaugural World Fire Congress. A nationally recognized authority on translating data into people-centered policy, her work spans research-informed leadership, workforce resilience, and firefighter safety.

Keynote Speaker
Chief John Alston
Fire Chief (Ret.), Vice-Chair BCOC
TUE8:30 – 9:30 AMKeynote
TUE–THUBook SigningSigning
Book Signing · Tue–Thu
Leading Fearlessly
Empowering fire officers to make impactful decisions in adverse situations. Following his Tuesday keynote, meet Chief Alston at the 10:30 AM book signing — copies available throughout the conference.
Chief John Alston Jr. brings more than 41 years of fire service experience and a people-centered leadership philosophy. He serves as Executive Coach and Trainer for the Fire Officer Trust (since October 2018) and is a certified John Maxwell Coach and Trainer, supporting business leaders, first responders, law enforcement, and emergency management leaders. Chief Alston served as Fire Chief of the New Haven (CT) Fire Department from October 2016 through his recent retirement, becoming the first fire chief in 155 years hired from outside the department. Before New Haven, he spent nearly 16 years with the Jersey City (NJ) Fire Department — rising to 1st Battalion Chief, and previously serving as Deputy Chief of Special Operations and Chief of Training, where he led all training for a "Metro" fire department and directed fire suppression and disaster planning across high-traffic assets including the Holland Tunnel, Ellis Island, and Liberty State Park. From 2012 to 2018 he also served as a Master Instructor with the International Association of Fire Fighters (IAFF). Chief Alston is Vice-Chair of the Black Chief Officers Committee (BCOC).
