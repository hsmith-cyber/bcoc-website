How to Declare Your Candidacy

Black Chief Officers Committee

Interested in running for a BCOC office? Submit your candidacy by email to the Elections Committee.

Send to elections@bcoc.us
CC hsmith@bcoc.us · glawrence@bcoc.us
Subject BCOC 2026 Candidacy — [Your Name]

Include in your email

  1. Full name
  2. Rank / title
  3. Department / agency
  4. Email & phone number
  5. Office sought — Chair, Vice-Chair, Secretary, Treasurer, or At-Large Director
  6. Years of BCOC membership
  7. A brief statement of interest (your vision & qualifications)
  8. Nominated by (if applicable)
📎Attach a current headshot and your resume/CV.
✉ Email My Candidacy

Confirm your membership eligibility before applying.
Questions? Contact Chief Greg Lawrence at glawrence@bcoc.us.
