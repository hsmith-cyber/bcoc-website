About Todd Stout

Todd Stout is the Founder and CEO of FirstWatch, a public safety technology company that turns the data already flowing through 9-1-1, EMS, fire and dispatch systems into real-time situational awareness — helping agencies see what is happening as it happens and make better, faster decisions. Guided by the company's mission of "Helping the Helpers," FirstWatch today serves hundreds of public safety agencies across North America.

A former EMT, paramedic and flight medic, Todd learned the business from the ground up — beginning in the Kansas City EMS system and later serving as Vice President and Chief Operating Officer of the Oklahoma City Emergency Medical Services Authority (EMSA), one of the nation's premier high-performance EMS systems. He also held leadership roles in ambulance operations and public safety software before founding the company (originally Stout Solutions) in 1998 and launching it as FirstWatch in 2002.

Todd is an active contributor to the national public safety community, with involvement in organizations including the International Association of EMS Chiefs, the National EMS Management Association, and the International Academies of Emergency Dispatch. In recognition of his leadership and lasting contributions to the field, he received the Pinnacle EMS Leadership Award. FirstWatch is proud to serve as the Lifting As We Climb sponsor of the BCOC 2026 Spouse / Partners Luncheon.
