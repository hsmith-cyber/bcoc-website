Pursuant to the authority vested in the Chair under the BCOC Bylaws, the 2026 Elections Committee has been formally constituted to administer the 2026 election process for the Black Chief Officers Committee. The Committee shall serve in accordance with Article V of the Bylaws and the duties set forth in Resolution 2026-[03].

Section 3Duties and Responsibilities

The 2026 Elections Committee shall perform all duties assigned to the Elections Committee under Article V of the BCOC Bylaws, including:

  • Oversee and administer the election of officers conducted during the 2026 annual BCOC conference.
  • Receive and process candidate submissions in accordance with bylaw deadlines and eligibility requirements.
  • Verify, in coordination with the appropriate BCOC officers, that candidates and voting members are in good standing as required by the Bylaws.
  • Coordinate the distribution and receipt of absentee and proxy ballots in accordance with Article V.
  • Ensure the integrity of the balloting process.
  • Count and certify the official election results.
  • Present the official results to the membership at the conclusion of the election process.

Governance Provisions

Section 4 · Eligibility and Compliance

The Committee shall conduct its work in conformity with Article V of the BCOC Bylaws, including provisions on voting qualifications, absentee ballot requests, proxy ballots, candidate eligibility, and certification of results.

Section 5 · Impartiality and Conflicts

Members shall act impartially and in the best interests of BCOC. Any member who becomes a candidate for elected office, or who has a conflict that may reasonably call impartiality into question, shall disclose that fact and recuse from related deliberations.

Section 6 · Records

The Committee shall maintain records of candidate submissions, ballot requests, ballot distribution, ballot receipt, tabulation, and certification of results, and shall provide a final written report to the Chair for inclusion in the organizational records.

Section 7 · Term

The Committee shall remain in effect until superseded by another resolution of the Chair or the Executive Board.
