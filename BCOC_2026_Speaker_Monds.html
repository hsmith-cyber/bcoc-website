About Chief Michael J. Monds

Chief Michael J. Monds is the Chief of Fire for the Syracuse (NY) Fire Department and a recognized fire service executive with 26 years of public safety experience. He has served as Fire Chief for nine years under two mayoral administrations and is the first Black Fire Chief in the department’s 150-year history. Chief Monds leads nearly 400 firefighters and civilian personnel protecting the City of Syracuse and the Central New York region. Under his leadership the department achieved the prestigious ISO Class 1 Public Protection Classification, modernized its apparatus fleet and operations, enhanced firefighter training and professional development, expanded leadership-development initiatives, and strengthened community partnerships. His administration has been recognized for historic progress in the recruitment, retention, and promotion of women and firefighters of color — producing the most diverse recruit classes and promotional opportunities in the department’s history — and reopened Engine Company 12 after more than three decades, restored Ladder Company staffing, and re-established a seven-person Rescue Company. Named the 2023 New York State Fire Chief of the Year (the first Syracuse chief to receive the distinction), Chief Monds also serves as a public safety consultant, executive recruiter, and leadership mentor, and believes the greatest legacy any fire chief can leave is preparing others to lead.

Closing Keynote

Straight From a Fire Lieutenant to the Fire Chief: Leadership Lessons, Breaking Barriers, and Preparing for the Chief’s Office

In this engaging, motivational closing keynote, Chief Monds shares his journey from Lieutenant to becoming the first Black Fire Chief in the 150-year history of the career Syracuse Fire Department — offering practical guidance on preparing for the chief’s office, mastering each assignment, understanding municipal government and labor relations, and leading with authenticity, resilience, and integrity.
